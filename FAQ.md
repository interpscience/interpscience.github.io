### How do I request a reciprocal-reviewer exception?

Every submission needs at least one author signed up as a reciprocal
reviewer. If no author qualifies, the submission form includes an option to
request an exception, along with a short statement of why. We review these
on a case-by-case basis and reply as soon as possible.

## Submissions

### When will the paper submission portal (OpenReview) open?

The submission portal is now live on OpenReview. You can submit your paper
[here](https://openreview.net/group?id=NeurIPS.cc/2026/Workshop/InterpScience). 

### Do references and appendices count toward the page limit?

No. The 5-page and 9-page limits cover the main text, figures and tables
included; references and an optional appendix go beyond them, following the
[NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook) and
[ICLR](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide) conventions. Reviewers read the appendix at their
discretion, so the main text has to carry the paper on its own.

### Does the workshop accept position papers or conceptual/perspective papers?

Yes, on both the short and long tracks. Our submission guidelines invite papers
that report new research or argue a position, and conceptual and perspective
papers fall under the second.

### Is work on architectures other than LLMs (e.g., RNNs, state space models) in scope?

Yes. Our topics of interest are methodological, so interpretability work on
recurrent neural networks, state space models, and other architectures fits the
call even though its framing mentions LLMs.

## Attending and presenting

### Do I need to register to present my paper?

Yes. Someone has to present each paper at the workshop, and that person needs a
pass. The type of pass depends on how they attend.

### What pass do I need to attend in person?

You need a “Sydney Workshops & Competitions” pass. This applies to every author
at the venue, including authors who are not presenting.

### What pass do I need to attend remotely?

You need a “Virtual Only” pass. This applies to remote attendees and remote
presenters.

### Can I present my poster remotely?

No. Our logistics do not allow remote poster presentations. All posters are
presented in person in Sydney. We have a small number of remote slots, and we
reserve them for contributed talks.

### None of the authors can travel to Sydney. Can someone else present our poster?

Yes. Any person can present the poster for you. That person needs a “Sydney
Workshops & Competitions” pass.

### Can I give my contributed talk remotely?

Yes. You can give a contributed talk in person or remotely. Your ability to
travel played no role in how we chose the talks.

### What happens if no one presents our paper?

Your paper stays accepted, and we list it as accepted on the workshop website.
It will not get a poster slot. You also do not need to register.

### Can I share a recorded talk?

Yes. Any author can upload a 6 to 8 minute recorded talk, and we will host it on
the workshop website. We strongly encourage a recording if your paper has no
contributed talk and no in-person poster. It is the best way for people to see
your work.

### Will there be presentations at the Atlanta or Paris locations?

No. The workshop has no satellite events. All talks and posters happen in Sydney
or online.
