NeurIPS 2026 · Sydney

Call for Papers

We invite submissions that engage with the scientific foundations of LLM interpretability— measurement, causality, falsifiability, and lessons from adjacent sciences.

Important dates

All deadlines are Anywhere on Earth (AoE).

Paper submission deadline
September 01, 2026
Author notification
September 25, 2026
Camera-ready deadline
November 01, 2026
Workshop
December 11 or 12, 2026

Topics of interest

We welcome work on topics including, but not limited to:

  • What it means to understand a model, and criteria for genuine explanation
  • Formal and mathematical frameworks for interpretability
  • Causal and interventional methods for grounding interpretability claims
  • Measurement validity, identifiability, and evaluation design
  • Falsifiability and experimental designs that distinguish mechanisms from artifacts
  • Transferable lessons from neuroscience, statistics, econometrics, and related fields
  • Pathways from interpretability findings to replicable science

Submission guidelines

  • We are inviting submissions of short (up to 5 pages) and long papers (up to 9 pages) outlining new research.
  • We will accept both ICLR and NeurIPS formats for submissions, but all camera ready papers must be in NeurIPS format. Find the style template for NeurIPS at https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers.
  • Per NeurIPS guidelines, we do not accept work that has been previously published in other conferences on machine learning or related fields, including NeurIPS 2026 main conference.
  • Accepted papers will be non-archival and presented as posters, with a subset selected for contributed talks.
  • All submissions will require at least one reciprocal reviewer, unless the paper has no authors qualified to be a reviewer or program committee member, in which case an exception can be requested.
  • Each reviewer will be asked to review 2-3 papers. Please sign up at https://forms.gle/DCUMr9WMWwn3pN6FA to express interest in being part of the program committee.

The submission portal will be linked here once available.
